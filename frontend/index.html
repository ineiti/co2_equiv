CO2 Equivalent Calculator

Describe an activity or item, e.g. "eating 150g of beef" or "forgot to turn off the office light for 2h". You will be given an estimation of kg of CO2 for this activity / item. Then it shows you the corresponding km one person can travel in a plane, car, or train.

Please be aware that this estimation may be very wrong!

This runs the Apertus 8B/Q4 model on a local server with limited hardware, so estimates can take a while to generate. Please be patient.
